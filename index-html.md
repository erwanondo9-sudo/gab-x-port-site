# Surface brief: Gab X-Port public site (home, professionnels, confidentialité, 404)

Scope: index.html home view and site chrome (header, footer, sign-in and other modals), plus professionnels.html, confidentialite.html and 404.html. Visitor mode: Persuade. The app views on index.html (annonces, favoris, historique, espace personnel, messagerie) inherit the world in Operate register.

Audience and job: clients looking for a professional on a corridor to Gabon, and professionals who should publish; equal weight. Action: search the catalogue (clients) and create a professional account to publish (pros). Proof on hand: five real port photographs (credited); no testimonials, counts or partners (launch stage, none may be invented). Constraints: app.js DOM hooks; CSP self-hosting; fast on mid-range Android over 4G (owner's stated failure condition).

Page structure (owner-approved, professional-marketplace model): Accueil, Annonces (app view), Professionnels (replaces services, fonctionnement, publier), Confidentialité; old URLs redirect.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Gab X-Port is a route canvas. Gabon is a live node wired to four partner ports, and every wire is a way to reach a professional. It refuses the category default: search bar on a stock photo, three icon cards, logo strip.

OWN-WORLD: The owner's pinned Refero n8n system, adapted. Void #0e0918 ground, surfaces #1a1624, #1b1728 and #2c2834, smoke borders #3e3a46. Ember gradient (#fd8925 to #ff0c00, 30deg) only on primary actions; electric current (#077ac7 to #6b21ef) on route wires, links and focus. Stack Sans Headline 300 display at line-height .9, Stack Sans Text body in ash #d1cece. Inset-glow cards (white hairline inset, ember lower edge), radius tiers 8/16/24, no outset drop shadows, ember and rose glows only in section corners. Port photographs clipped at 24px like product screenshots.

STORY: In one screen the visitor sees the four corridors wired to Gabon, can search immediately, and sees the second door for professionals. Then: how a contact happens (a wired three-node flow), what each corridor offers (ports), the latest real listings (the catalogue at work; listings without photos show their corridor port), what Gab X-Port does not do, the professional offer, help.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left five columns: h1 "Le Gabon, relié à ses partenaires." at about 80px, one sentence, a working search (field, corridor select, ember Rechercher), then a quiet second door "Vous êtes professionnel ? Publier vos services". Right seven columns, bleeding off the right edge: the route canvas, Gabon node and four port nodes joined by animated electric wires; each port node is a button that opens that corridor's listings.

FORM: Owner-pinned reference, position 1 of 1: Refero "n8n design system", carried as an interface language. seed:67ea0c0193a3de00 (roll recorded; the owner-pinned Refero reference stays the direction).

SIGNATURE INTERACTION AND RISK: Pulses travel along the wires toward Gabon; hovering or focusing a port node lights its wire and shows its port photo. Risk: a dark tech world reading as a developer tool to traders and transporters; held off by warm ember light, real port photographs and plain French. Phone: the canvas becomes a vertical route list with the same wires, no blur filters, photos lazy-loaded.
